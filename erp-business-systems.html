Datum Advisory

ERP readiness, software selection and implementation support

An ERP investment connects sales, purchasing, stock, manufacturing and finance. Datum helps you establish how those connections need to work and prepare the business to make the change.

Before committing to a system

A growing business may have outgrown its tools. An existing system may be underused, or departments may have built separate workarounds. We examine the processes, information and decisions behind the request for change.

The starting point is an agreed need and a realistic view of what the business can take on.

Where I can contribute

  • Review business needs, readiness and existing arrangements.
  • Translate operational requirements into a brief suppliers can respond to.
  • Support software sourcing, evaluation and selection.
  • Bring process owners and technical specialists together.
  • Help organise data preparation, responsibilities, testing and training.
  • Review dependencies, priorities and decisions during implementation.
  • Assess an existing programme and establish practical next steps.

Business understanding alongside systems expertise

Sales commitments affect engineering, purchasing, production and cash flow. A system needs to reflect those relationships. My role can include interpreting needs between departments and helping leadership understand the implications of the available options.

I work with internal teams, suppliers and specialist consultants according to the scope agreed.

Experience delivering integration

Earlier in my career, I built support for a SAP manufacturing proposal and formed a local team of process experts, corporate systems specialists and external consultants. Together we implemented SAP manufacturing in three equipment factories over around 12 months.

The programme integrated sales order processing, manufacturing and finance. It also created opportunities for further process improvement.
