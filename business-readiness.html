Business readiness, priorities and change support

A business can have plenty of improvement ideas and still struggle to make progress. I help owners and management teams establish what matters, what is preventing progress and what needs to happen first.

Bring the priorities together

Growth, customer demands and operational pressures can pull departments in different directions. We work through the aims, evidence and constraints to develop a shared view of the next steps.

This can include reviewing responsibilities, decisions, management information, skills and the practical capacity to deliver change.

The six pillars

Datum’s six pillars provide a way to examine the conditions supporting improvement.

  • Governance
  • Process Ownership
  • Systems Discipline
  • Data Standards
  • Capability
  • Controls

Psychological safety and AI run across all six pillars as they become more integrated into business activity and more important to successful change. People need to be able to raise concerns, question assumptions and share what they know. AI needs clear ownership, suitable data, appropriate skills and effective oversight. Cyber security is also considered across the pillars.

The scope of a review depends on the business and the decision it faces.

Support at different stages

A customer questionnaire or request for ISO 9001 may expose a need for clearer processes and evidence. A systems investment may expose gaps in data ownership or management decisions. A stalled programme may need priorities and responsibilities resolved before more development work begins.

I help connect the immediate request with the wider decisions, then agree a proportionate response.

What we could agree to deliver

A structured review, a facilitated discussion, written findings, a sequence of prioritised improvements or ongoing advisory support for a defined period.

The aim is to leave leadership better able to decide, allocate resources and follow through.
