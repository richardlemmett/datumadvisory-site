Independent technology, automation and AI advice

You hear about AI and automation, but it can be difficult to judge what would help your business. I help you examine the work, identify useful opportunities and understand what introducing a solution would involve.

Start with the work

We look at the activity you want to improve: who does it, what information it uses, where judgement is needed and what a useful result would be. This gives us a basis for considering software, automation, AI or changes to the process itself.

Questions worth resolving

  • What problem would the proposed change solve?
  • Is the information suitable and who owns it?
  • What should remain subject to human review?
  • How would the solution connect with existing tools?
  • Who would maintain it, control access and check its output?
  • What are the costs, dependencies and consequences of failure?

Independent support with the decision

I can help assess a proposal, develop requirements, compare options or plan a limited trial. Findings can also identify process or data improvements needed before an investment will be useful.

IT infrastructure, cyber security and supplier arrangements can be reviewed in the context of the wider business, with specialist input coordinated where required.

Technology choices have lasting consequences

A nuclear equipment tender provides an example of evaluating control technology against the application’s requirements. Working with the client, I helped establish an analogue alternative that avoided the software and version-management obligations of the proposed digital controls.
