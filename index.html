How I help

Start with what your business needs.

You may be planning a major investment, working around an awkward process, or trying to fix something important. Datum can help you establish what to do next.

Prepare for change

Before committing to new systems, understand what needs to be in place for the change to work.

  • ERP requirements and preparation

Investigate a systems or data problem and agree a proportionate way to put it right.

  • Small business tools and automation
  • Access, Power Query and SharePoint solutions
