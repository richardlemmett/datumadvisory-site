“Who helps us make the right decisions?”

When systems responsibility is spread between managers, suppliers and individual experts, decisions can become disconnected. Datum helps leadership clarify business aims and connect them with operational needs, technology choices, costs and delivery.

The role can be a sounding board for a particular decision, regular advisory support, or an agreed fractional leadership responsibility.

Focus the role around your needs.

  • Helping shape organisational strategy where operations, information and technology are central
  • Translating strategic aims into a practical roadmap, with ownership, dependencies and investment priorities
  • Aligning system architecture and proposed solutions with business direction
  • Investment choices and software, subscription and cloud reviews
  • Supplier research, shortlisting, evaluation and engagement, followed by relationship and service reviews
  • ERP, CRM, MES and planning programme oversight, translating between business and technical teams
  • Cyber security, continuity and information risks, coordinating specialists as needed
  • Practical AI adoption and developing internal capability

Responsibilities, decision authority, availability and reporting are agreed explicitly. Ongoing advisory is distinct from an operational helpdesk or an emergency-response service.

A manageable commitment.

Retainers or agreed days provide a regular rhythm for priorities, decisions and progress reviews. You receive clear written outputs and a record of relevant risks and next steps.

Help people make the change work.

New systems and processes can change responsibilities, team boundaries and familiar ways of working. Datum helps you understand those impacts, involve the people affected and plan communication, training and support around the work they need to do.

We help establish who needs to be involved, how decisions will be made and where concerns or competing priorities need attention. Clear responsibilities and practical support help teams take ownership of the change.
