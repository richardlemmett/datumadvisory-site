Case Studies

Datum Advisory’s work comes from decades of fixing real problems in real businesses.

Different industries, different systems, with the same pattern every time: something important isn’t working properly, and people are quietly working around it. Over time, that creates fragile systems that sprawl in ways nobody intended, work-arounds or even dangerous situations that folks have simply become accustomed to.

The aim is always the same: keep it simple, fix the underlying issue, minimise risk, and avoid unnecessary cost.

Below are a few examples of solutions in the genesis of Datum Advisory that made a material difference, but the real benefits come when change is driven in a safe, sequenced and governed way to align with an organisation’s strategic objectives.



Factory Documentation
A manufacturing business was held back by a 1980s‑era system.

Moving to the wider enterprise’s SAP system was inevitable, but the real constraint was factory documentation; batching work because producing smaller packets was too slow.

By using SAP’s production controls properly, documentation for smaller batches could be generated quickly, improving flow through the factory and unlocking growth.
