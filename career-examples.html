Selected experience from Richard’s career

These examples come from my career before Datum and from subsequent business systems work. They show how I approach requirements, bring people together and connect decisions with delivery.

SAP manufacturing across three factories

I identified an opportunity to introduce SAP manufacturing and worked with colleagues from related factories to develop a proposal. Once authorised, I formed a local working team of process experts, supported by corporate systems specialists and external consultants.

Together we successfully implemented SAP manufacturing in three equipment factories over around 12 months, with no major issues recalled. Sales order processing, manufacturing and finance were brought into an integrated arrangement.

The work involved building support for the investment and organising the business expertise needed for implementation. Further production-order improvement work illustrates how the resulting capability could be used.

A common tendering process with product flexibility

Working with a UK-wide team, I helped define standard tendering processes and documentation that could accommodate different products and tender production tools. We also worked with IT and developers on a custom SAP extension providing CRM functionality.

I then developed costing and tender tools for selected product ranges. These accommodated standard and engineered-to-order equipment, from small individual items to large framework tenders.

The work connected national standards with the practical requirements of individual businesses and products.

A nationwide telecommunications supply programme

A client developing a mobile telecommunications co-location programme asked for help with an innovative supply arrangement. I coordinated our supply scope through discussions, design workshops, tendering rounds and work with the client’s partners and subcontractors.

Many sites needed the usual single equipment assembly divided into ground-level supply and metering equipment for the local distribution network operator, and rooftop distribution equipment for the mobile operators. We developed a catalogue of custom equipment for the programme.

The project led to a production line being activated and staffed. I managed the resulting long-term project, including planning, stock, complex delivery logistics and supporting business systems.

For later upgrades, I planned, mobilised and managed a team of site operatives completing modifications across more than 400 sites throughout much of the UK, on time and within budget.

Control technology for a nuclear application

During tender development for a nuclear facility, the proposed electrical equipment would ordinarily have used microprocessor-based controls. I reviewed several hundred documents and standards relating to nuclear-island requirements and worked through the implications with the client.

We agreed an analogue control configuration that avoided the software, version-management and associated security obligations of the proposed microprocessor controls.

The work connected detailed requirements interpretation with a practical equipment configuration agreed with the client.

Interim reporting and operational information tools

To improve visibility alongside an existing ERP, I developed Excel reporting tools and an Access-based stock management application. The Access application replaced multiple spreadsheets and linked to ERP data, supporting serial-numbered unit tracking for upstream logistics.

The ERP remained the system of record. The application supplemented it with more accessible, granular operational views. Configurable lists, searches and filters helped users see relevant information, with context-menu actions adapted to their role.

The Excel work used Power Query data pipelines, data models, consistent layouts and managed refresh features. Individual applications included tailored views, defined filters, explanatory comments and analysis.

The approach was to address specific operational needs while retaining the core business system.

Waste, resource efficiency and environmental data

At Schneider Electric, my responsibilities included environmental reporting, energy and emissions monitoring, regulatory review and permitting. With my team, we established waste optimisation and recycling arrangements for more than 40 waste streams, most revenue generating or cost neutral.

Root cause investigation at a new hospital

Investigation of repeated component failures identified network overvoltage events. I planned and completed testing and repairs across 115 distribution panels while the client arranged the transformer tap adjustment. All identified issues were resolved to the client’s satisfaction.
