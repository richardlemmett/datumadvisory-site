Engineering, commercial and business systems experience

My experience helps me understand the detail behind business decisions and work with people across different functions. The areas below reflect work undertaken across my career, including technical, management and systems roles.

Commercial and management

Tendering and bid processes; commercial terms development, interpretation and negotiation; complex product costing; offer preparation; contract management; technical requirements interpretation; technical commentary and specification development.

Business management systems, including experience with ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and the former OHSAS 18001; quality control and inspection; Lean manufacturing and TPS; Six Sigma; health and safety; environmental management controls; sustainability; regulatory compliance and waste management.

Manufacturing and engineering

Injection moulding, compression moulding, steel fabrication and stamping, copper manufacturing, powder coating, plating operations, electrical assembly, CNC machinery, stock control, electrical and mechanical inspection, and 2D/3D design.

Experience also includes master data management, product lifecycle management, IT infrastructure and building systems.

Electrical products and technologies

Low- and medium-voltage switchgear and distribution systems, transformers, motor control, PLCs and critical AC/DC secure power systems. Battery technology experience includes nickel-cadmium, VRLA and Planté systems.

Markets and applications

Electricity distribution, including the UK DNO, iDNO and ICP market and overseas network operators; onshore and offshore oil and gas; nuclear; gas turbines; UK Ministry of Defence applications; buildings; seaports and airports; telecommunications; industrial manufacturing and hospital distribution systems.

Experience also includes exporting electrical equipment to destinations around the world.

Business applications and development

Experience with SAP, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Salesforce, EFACS, ProgressPlus and Therefore. Development work includes Microsoft Word, Excel, Access and Outlook applications, Power Query, data models, Power Pivot analysis and HTML websites.

The depth and nature of involvement varies by subject. We establish the expertise needed for an engagement and coordinate specialist input where appropriate.

How this helps a client

A customer specification can affect design, costing, purchasing, production, inspection, documentation and delivery. Understanding those connections helps uncover assumptions and develop requirements that different teams can work with.

This experience provides a practical basis for advisory work on priorities, systems and organisational change.
