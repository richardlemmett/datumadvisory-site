Tendering, product costing and commercial process support

A good offer needs to connect what the customer asks for with what the business can supply, at a cost and risk it understands. I help develop the processes, information and tools that support that decision.

When commercial work becomes difficult to control

Costing may depend on individual knowledge. Specifications can run to hundreds of documents. Quotations may be assembled differently by each person, with assumptions lost when the order reaches engineering or production.

I bring experience from tendering, engineering, manufacturing and systems to examine how those activities fit together.

Where I can help

  • Develop tendering, qualification, review and approval processes.
  • Interpret technical requirements and prepare commentary or clarifications.
  • Develop specifications and record agreed exceptions.
  • Build costing methods, models and tools for standard and engineered-to-order products.
  • Improve quotation and commercial offer preparation.
  • Support commercial terms development, interpretation and negotiation, coordinating legal advice where needed.
  • Develop contract records, change controls and handover arrangements.

Consistency with room for product differences

Shared processes can improve control while retaining flexibility for different equipment and tender sizes. Earlier work included national tendering standards and tools handling individual products through to large framework tenders.
