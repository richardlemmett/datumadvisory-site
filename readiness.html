Clarity before transformation.

Before investing in a new system or digital initiative, understand the conditions your business needs for it to work.

Let’s talk about your business ↗Explore how we help

Readiness is about the organisation.

Technology depends on leadership, people, processes, information and clear ownership. A readiness engagement uses conversations and relevant evidence to explore where these conditions support your plans and where attention is needed.

The scope is tailored to the decision ahead. Datum’s developing Six Pillars framework informs this work; the approach and outputs are agreed before commissioning.

A practical written basis for action.

  • Findings supported by observations and evidence
  • Strengths, gaps and material dependencies
  • Prioritised recommendations and a manageable next step
  • Decisions and responsibilities that need to be resolved

This can support ERP, CRM, automation or AI planning. The aim is to improve the conditions for success, rather than produce a pass-or-fail score.

Help people make the change work.

New systems and processes can change responsibilities, team boundaries and familiar ways of working. Datum helps you understand those impacts, involve the people affected and plan communication, training and support around the work they need to do.

We help establish who needs to be involved, how decisions will be made and where concerns or competing priorities need attention. Clear responsibilities and practical support help teams take ownership of the change.
